# Product Context: AI Resume Checker

## Problem Statement
Job seekers struggle with:
- **ATS Rejection**: 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS systems before human review
- **Generic Resumes**: One-size-fits-all resumes don't stand out to employers
- **Skills Gap**: Unclear understanding of what skills are missing for target roles
- **Format Issues**: Poor formatting reduces readability and ATS compatibility
- **Keyword Optimization**: Not knowing which keywords to include for specific industries/roles

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Job seekers (entry to mid-level professionals)
- **Secondary**: Career coaches, resume writers, HR professionals
- **Tertiary**: Students preparing for job market entry

## Solution Approach
An intelligent resume analysis platform that:
1. **Uploads & Parses** resumes in multiple formats
2. **Analyzes Content** using AI to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities
3. **Provides Scores** for ATS compatibility, keyword optimization, and overall quality
4. **Generates Recommendations** with specific, actionable improvement suggestions
5. **Compares Against Jobs** to show compatibility with specific roles

## User Experience Goals
- **Simple Upload**: Drag-and-drop or click-to-upload interface
- **Quick Analysis**: Results within 30 seconds
- **Clear Feedback**: Visual scores and easy-to-understand recommendations
- **Actionable Insights**: Specific suggestions, not generic advice
- **Progress Tracking**: Ability to see improvements over time

## Key Features (MVP)
1. **Resume Upload**: Support PDF, DOCX, TXT formats
2. **Content Analysis**: Extract and analyze text content
3. **ATS Scoring**: Evaluate ATS compatibility
4. **Keyword Analysis**: Identify missing or weak keywords
5. **Format Assessment**: Check structure and formatting
6. **Recommendations**: Generate improvement suggestions
7. **Results Dashboard**: Display analysis results clearly

## Success Metrics
- **User Engagement**: Time spent analyzing results
- **Resume Quality**: Improvement in ATS scores after recommendations
- **User Satisfaction**: Feedback on recommendation usefulness
- **Processing Speed**: Analysis completion time
- **Accuracy**: Relevance of generated recommendations

## Competitive Advantage
- **Multi-format Support**: Handle various resume formats seamlessly
- **Industry-Specific**: Tailored recommendations for different fields
- **Real-time Analysis**: Fast processing with immediate results
- **Comprehensive Scoring**: Multiple evaluation dimensions
- **Actionable Feedback**: Specific, implementable suggestions
